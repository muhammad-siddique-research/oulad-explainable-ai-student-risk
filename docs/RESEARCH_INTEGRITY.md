# Research integrity, interpretation and disclosure

- OULAD is an **authentic historical UK source**. Data-source authenticity is distinct from completion of the proposed analysis.
- The repository is a **software/research-stage implementation**. Until real OULAD model outputs are generated, there are **no valid empirical coefficients, algorithm performance results, or case-level SHAP estimates to report**.
- Local synthetic tests verify programming invariants only; their numbers are deliberately fictitious and cannot support journal tables.
- The design is **quantitative**, not mixed methods; no qualitative interviews or matched narrative records are part of this source.
- No robot, teacher-facing AI assistant, real-time feedback mechanism, randomized assignment, or intervention effect is observed.
- Statistical associations, held-out predictive performance, and model attributions answer different questions. None alone establishes causality.
- Course-presentation groups and repeated learners introduce dependence; holdout policy and effective group sample size must be reported.
- The models are not student-facing decision services; do not deploy for consequential decisions without prospective validation and governance.
- Before public release or journal submission, confirm manuscript citations, licensing, funding and affiliation acknowledgments, author contributions, conflicts of interest, and any generative-AI disclosures required by the intended venue.
- Keep real-data extracts, private identity fields, and learned predictions outside version control. The original OULAD dataset is CC BY 4.0; repository MIT License covers only new software.
