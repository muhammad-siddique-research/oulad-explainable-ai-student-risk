## Research-integrity notes

- The **original dataset publication** must be cited whenever OULAD is used; citation of this repository alone is insufficient.
- The repository's own working project is **not** falsely assigned a journal, volume, page range, external DOI or peer-reviewed status.
- The 2026 Wu et al. article reports evaluation themes substantially overlapping early-window group validation, calibration and fair-alerting. Accordingly, this project does **not** claim to be the first OULAD early-warning, calibration, XGBoost or fairness study. Check the publisher's August 2026 correction notice to that article before drawing detailed numeric comparisons with it.
- In text, distinguish archival empirical findings from methodological interpretation and proposed future tests. References are not proof of the present dataset's experimental identification.
- Before journal submission, resolve manuscript-wide in-text citation correspondence, source metadata and any journal-specific disclosures; references should only be retained when used.
